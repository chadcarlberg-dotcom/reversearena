            The concept

            A 600-seat venue where the audience rotates in the middle of a ring-shaped stage, revealing four distinct stages, each run by a student team competing against the rest.

            600SeatsRotating central audience
            4StagesOne surrounding performance ring
            4Student teamsCompeting against one another

            The room

            One room, four stages around the audience

            • 600 seatsOn a 27-metre platform that turns left or right to face any of the four stages
            • LED walls on a 360° trackTwo 90° sections, 21.2 m each, open to reveal the stage that is playing
            • Corner modeTwo stages join into one wide stage
            • A multi-use houseRemovable seating, a rotating downstage thrust and a rotating overhead rig for lighting, sound and effects
            • The platform turns to whichever stage is playing; the others are reset behind the walls
            • No scene changes in front of the audience, and no dark weeks between productions
            • The second half goes wherever the room’s vote sends it
